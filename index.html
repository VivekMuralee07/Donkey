i Donkey rules
  • Players: 2 to 5. Bots can fill empty seats. Choose Easy (random cards), Medium (plays it safe) or Hard (remembers cards, plans ahead and learns from how people play).
  • Dealing: 2 players get 39 random cards (20 and 19). 3 players get 39 random cards (13 each). 4 players get the full deck (13 each). 5 players get the full deck (10 or 11 each). The Ace of Spades is always dealt.
  • Start: whoever holds the Ace of Spades plays it first. Play then goes clockwise.
  • Card order: Ace is highest, then K, Q, J, 10 down to 2.
  • Follow suit: if you have a card of the suit that was led, you must play one.
  • Everyone follows suit: when every player has played a card of that suit, the round ends and those cards are set aside. The player with the highest card leads the next round.
  • No card of that suit: you play any card and the round stops right there. The player with the highest card of the led suit picks up all the cards on the table and leads next.
  • Winning: the first player to run out of cards wins. This is checked when a round ends. Among the other players, the one left with the most cards is the Donkey 🫏.
  • Early finish: if a player's cards are all one suit, and they must play that suit, and the next player has none of that suit, the game ends and the next player wins.
  • After the game: tap Back to lobby when you are ready. There you can change avatars, add bots and deal again.
  • Voice talk: when two or more real players are in the room, tap 🎙 to join voice. Tap the green 🎤 to mute or unmute your microphone, and 📴 to leave voice. Bots are not part of voice.
  • Chat: tap the 💬 button to type messages to the other players in your room.
  • Voice chat: tap the blue 🎧 button to join your group's Discord voice channel. Paste the invite link on the room screen first.
